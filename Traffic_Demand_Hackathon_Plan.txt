
TRAFFIC DEMAND PREDICTION HACKATHON - COMPLETE PROJECT PLAN

PROJECT OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Build an AI/ML system capable of predicting traffic demand using spatial, temporal,
weather, and road-network information. The evaluation metric is R² score, so the goal
is to maximize predictive accuracy while ensuring the solution generalizes across the
distribution shift between Day 48 and Day 49.

====================================================
PHASE 1: PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING
====================================================

Key Challenge:
- Significant distribution shift exists between Day 48 and Day 49.
- Most training samples belong to Day 48.
- Day 49 contains fewer observations.
- The model must generalize to unseen temporal conditions.

Target:
- Predict the "demand" column.
- Demand values lie in the range (0,1].

Evaluation:
Score = max(0, 100 × R²(actual, predicted))

Success Factors:
1. Strong temporal feature engineering.
2. Strong geospatial feature engineering.
3. Proper validation strategy.
4. Ensemble modeling.
